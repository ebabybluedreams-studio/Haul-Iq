FAQ

Common questions.

Do I need to be tech-savvy to use this? +
Not at all. If you can use a smartphone, you can use Haul IQ. It takes about 5 minutes to set up your profile and you're running. No installs, no downloads — works in any browser.
Does it work for all freight types? +
Yes — Dry Van, Flatbed, Reefer, LTL, Hotshot, and Tanker. The AI adjusts its load scoring, route recommendations, and compliance checks based on your specific freight type.
Is this a real-time load board or an AI tool? +
Haul IQ is an AI intelligence layer. It analyzes loads and gives you smart recommendations. For live load posting, you still use DAT or Truckstop — Haul IQ tells you which ones are actually worth booking based on your specific operation.
Can I use it for my whole small fleet? +
Pro handles up to 5 trucks. Fleet Commander handles up to 15. Each truck gets its own status tracking, compliance monitoring, and route optimization.
Can I cancel anytime? +
Yes. No contracts, no cancellation fees. Cancel with one click from your account settings. You keep access until the end of your billing period.
Is the compliance advice legally binding? +
No — Haul IQ gives you plain-English explanations of DOT/FMCSA regulations to help you stay informed and flag issues early. For serious compliance situations, always verify with a licensed compliance officer or attorney.
